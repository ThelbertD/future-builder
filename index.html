Questions

Before you claim a slot

Can I hire you for just one service? +

Yes. Plenty of clients start with just a website, just video edits, or just social. Every service is scoped and quoted on its own. Tell me what you need on the call and you'll get a price for exactly that.

Do I need to already have GoHighLevel? +

Only if you want the CRM and automation side. If you have it, I'll build inside your account. If you don't, I'll guide you to the right plan, or build on tools you already pay for. You fully own everything either way.

Do I need to be technical to run it? +

No. Everything is handed over with a recorded Loom walkthrough and simple docs. If you can check email and approve a message, you can run it.

How many revisions do I get? +

Revisions are built into every stage, and we agree the scope up front so nobody is guessing. On top of that, every project comes with 30 days of support after launch for tweaks and fixes.

What do you need from me to start? +

A short kickoff call, access to the accounts involved, and your offer details, plus any raw footage or brand files if we're doing design or video. That's it. I handle the building, you handle approvals.

Why only a few slots a month? +

Because I do the work personally rather than farming it out. Taking on too many projects at once would mean cutting corners, and the on-time guarantee wouldn't hold.
